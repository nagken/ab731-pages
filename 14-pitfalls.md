# AB-731 Pitfalls and Gotchas

> Wrong-answer traps the exam loves to set.

## 1. Adoption metrics != value

Seats activated, prompts/user - these are **vanity** metrics. Pair with productivity + business outcome.

## 2. Free Microsoft Copilot is NOT M365 Copilot

Different product, different governance, different data flow. Web Copilot has no tenant grounding.

## 3. M365 Copilot license is a separate add-on

Having M365 E3/E5 alone does NOT include Copilot. M365 Copilot is a per-user add-on.

## 4. Skipping data hygiene before flipping on Copilot

Oversharing in SharePoint = data leak through Copilot. Always run the **Microsoft 365 Copilot Optimization Assessment** first.

## 5. Pilot without champions stalls

The #1 reason pilots fail is no champions network. Establish 1:50 ratio before rollout.

## 6. One-shot training fails

People forget. Plan continuous training, prompt library, monthly tips.

## 7. Confusing Copilot Studio and Azure AI Foundry

- **Copilot Studio** = low-code, Power Platform style, built-in topics + connectors.
- **Azure AI Foundry** = code-first, model catalog + prompt flow + eval.

## 8. Confusing M365 Copilot Chat and the licensed M365 Copilot

- M365 Copilot **Chat** is the web-grounded chat - free with most M365 subscriptions.
- M365 Copilot (full) adds Copilot inside Word/Excel/PPT/Outlook/Teams + tenant grounding.

## 9. Skipping Responsible AI Impact Assessments

Each new AI use case needs an Impact Assessment for high-risk scenarios. Without it, regulatory and reputational risk.

## 10. Confusing Microsoft RAI Standard v2 with EU AI Act

- **RAI Standard** = Microsoft's internal framework, publicly shared.
- **EU AI Act** = legally binding regulation in EU.

## 11. Forgetting Microsoft Copyright Commitment

For commercial Copilot output, Microsoft offers **IP indemnification** if customers follow recommended guardrails.

## 12. Copilot Studio is NOT free

Pricing is per-message capacity unit. Plan capacity before rollout.

## 13. Azure OpenAI is NOT pay-by-month per user

Consumption-based (per token) or PTU (provisioned throughput) - quota planning matters.

## 14. M365 Copilot != training the foundation model

Microsoft does NOT use your tenant data to train the foundation models. Frequent CFO/CIO question.

## 15. CFO needs ROI; CIO needs governance; CEO needs vision

Don't pitch the same deck to all stakeholders.
