# AB-731 Hands-On Labs

> Strategic exercises for an AI Transformation Leader (no Azure portal expertise required).

## Foundational

1. **Read the latest Work Trend Index** - [microsoft.com/worklab/work-trend-index](https://www.microsoft.com/worklab/work-trend-index). Note 5 stats you can quote to executives.
2. **Microsoft 365 Copilot Adoption Center tour** - [adoption.microsoft.com/copilot](https://adoption.microsoft.com/copilot/). Download Champion's Guide template.
3. **Try Microsoft Copilot (free)** - at [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com), do 5 business tasks (summarize an article, draft an email, brainstorm).

## Domain 1 - Business Value

4. **Build a one-page AI vision** - write a 30-word vision plus 3 KPIs you can measure in 90 days.
5. **Use-case prioritization workshop** - list 10 candidate AI use cases for your org; place each on a value-vs-risk 2x2.
6. **Compute ROI** - pick 1 use case; estimate time saved, hourly cost, license cost; compute break-even users.
7. **Stakeholder talking points** - write a 3-bullet talking-point memo for CEO, CFO, CIO, CISO.

## Domain 2 - Microsoft AI Apps

8. **Map portfolio to roles** - for each role in your org (knowledge worker, seller, support agent, finance, dev), pick the right Microsoft AI tool.
9. **Watch the Copilot Studio overview** - [aka.ms/copilotstudio](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-copilot/microsoft-copilot-studio).
10. **Tour Azure AI Foundry** - [ai.azure.com](https://ai.azure.com); browse model catalog; note GPT, embedding, vision models.
11. **Build vs buy decision** - for one custom workflow, decide: M365 Copilot extension, Copilot Studio, or Azure AI Foundry.

## Domain 3 - Implementation + Adoption

12. **Run the Microsoft 365 Copilot Optimization Assessment** - review SharePoint sharing posture for your tenant.
13. **Draft a Champion charter** - define champion role, time commitment, training plan, recognition.
14. **Write an RAI Impact Assessment** - pick 1 high-risk use case (e.g. customer-facing chatbot); fill the Microsoft RAI Impact Assessment template.
15. **Adoption KPI dashboard** - define 5 KPIs (adoption + productivity + outcome) you'd track monthly.
16. **Pilot plan** - write a 1-page pilot plan: scope, users, KPIs, timeline, exit criteria.
17. **Change-management calendar** - plan 12 weeks of comms: announce, train, share wins, survey.

## Reading

18. **Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework: AI scenarios** - [aka.ms/CAF/AI](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/scenarios/ai/).
19. **Microsoft Responsible AI Standard v2** - read the publicly available PDF.
20. **EU AI Act overview** - read Microsoft's commitment blog posts.
